About us

About Complete Performance

Complete Performance is an online-based platform providing strength and conditioning, rehabilitation and sport science services to athletes and coaches.

We support recreational and professional athletes, fitness enthusiasts and sport professionals. Our approach combines practical experience with sport science to improve performance, guide rehabilitation, monitor training and support evidence-based decision making.

Our philosophy is simple: use reliable assessment, individual context and the best available evidence to translate science into daily practice.
